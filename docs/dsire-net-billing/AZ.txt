--- DSIRE BLOCK ---
Residential (APS, TEP, UNS) & Small Commercial (TEP, UNS): Excess generation
credited at an ACC-approved rate, phasing down to an avoided cost rate.
As of February 25, 2026, current export compensation rates are 6.171 cents/kWh
(APS), 5.13 cents/kWh (TEP), and 6.12 cents/kWh (UNS). NEG is carried forward
and paid out at the end of the annual period if the credit balance exceeds a
certain amount.

--- RETAIL CONTEXT ---
APS residential average rate ~12.8¢/kWh (2026, ElectricChoice / UtilityCheck).
Standard R-1 plan effective ~14.1¢/kWh. AZ statewide residential avg ~15.5¢.

--- NOTES ---
- BTM customer-generator tariff only — residential + small commercial (size-capped).
- Flat annual rate, no TOU structure on the export side.
- RCP framework: step-down ≤10%/yr, locked 10yr at each customer's interconnection date.
- APS dominates AZ load → APS rate anchors the state signal; TEP/UNS slightly lower.
- Ratio used in NET_BILLING_EXPORT_RATIO: 6.171 / 12.8 = 0.482 → encoded 0.48.
